# Sprite credits

## Robots (`robots.png`, `robots.json`)

The 14 robots come from the [Pixel Art Atlas](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Angais/pixel-art-atlas-1.4m)
(`Angais/pixel-art-atlas-1.4m`, `train` split). `robots.json` gives each one's `rowId` in the dataset. Their near-black
pixels were recolored to Donut's outline color; nothing else changed. ADR 0006 has the details, and
`make sprites` rebuilds them.
